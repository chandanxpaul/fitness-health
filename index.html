Calculation details

    Activity level reference

    TDEE is estimated as BMR multiplied by a factor for your typical weekly activity.

    Activity level Description Maintenance
    Sedentary little or no exercise BMR × 1.2
    Lightly active light exercise / sports 1–3 days / week BMR × 1.375
    Moderately active moderate exercise / sports 3–5 days / week BMR × 1.55
    Very active intensive exercise / sports 6–7 days / week BMR × 1.725

    Formulas: revised Harris–Benedict (Roza & Shizgal, 1984). Estimates only — not medical advice.
